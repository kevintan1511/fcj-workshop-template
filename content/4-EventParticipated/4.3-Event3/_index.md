# Summary Report: “FCAJ x FPTU: Cloud Mastery 1”

### Event Information

- **Event Name:** FCAJ x FPTU: Cloud Mastery 1
- **Date & Time:** Saturday, 14/11/2026 (09:00 – 12:00)
- **Location:** 26th Floor, Bitexco Financial Tower, No. 2 Hai Trieu, Ben Nghe Ward, District 1, Ho Chi Minh City (AWS Vietnam Office)
- **Organizers:** Joint event organized by First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ) and FPT University (FPTU)
- **Registration Method:** Direct registration via FPTU Student Portal
- **Role:** Attendee / FPTU Student & Intern

### Event Objectives

- Establish an inspiring forum for connecting, networking, and sharing cloud computing knowledge specifically tailored for FPT University students.
- Update practical insights regarding enterprise cloud architectures and modern Enterprise AI applications in production settings.
- Provide career orientation, resolving questions about professional roadmaps and essential skills needed in the Cloud & AI sector.
- Facilitate engaging community bonding via interactive technical quizzes and introducing upcoming community milestones (AWS Student Community Day Vietnam 2026).

### Event Highlights & Sessions

#### 1. Registration & Organization
This official event was organized specifically for FPT University students at the AWS Vietnam Office (26th Floor, Bitexco). Priority capacity at the office was limited, and portal registration closed automatically upon reaching quota, drawing enthusiastic participation from students across Saigon.

#### 2. Vibrant Atmosphere at the AWS Vietnam Office
On Saturday morning (14/11/2026), the event unfolded successfully with an energetic crowd of young tech students. The modern AWS office space provided an ideal environment for hands-on technical exchanges and direct networking.

#### 3. Interactive “Never-ending” Q&A Session
A central highlight of the event was the dynamic Q&A exchange, characterized as *"never-ending"* due to the volume of practical, insightful inquiries raised by students:
- Ranging from career positioning, interview preparation, and internship success strategies to deep architectural questions regarding Cloud, Serverless, and AI model serving.
- AWS speakers and specialists provided comprehensive answers, sharing valuable real-world experiences from enterprise projects.

#### 4. Lively Minigame Quiz Session
In addition to technical presentations and panel discussions, an interactive online quiz contest at the end of the workshop was enthusiastically received. Practical scenario questions (such as distinguishing Enterprise AI characteristics from Academic AI prototypes) reinforced key takeaways in an engaging manner.

#### 5. Teaser for Upcoming Community Events
The organizers officially previewed the upcoming community event: an exclusive student cloud workshop titled **AWS Student Community Day Vietnam 2026**.

### Key Takeaways

- **Enterprise AI vs. Academic AI:** Enterprise AI focuses on solving tangible business problems, operating on dynamically changing data with high availability, security, and scalability, rather than optimizing static accuracy metrics on benchmark datasets.
- **Value of In-Person Community Networking:** Attending on-site events at the AWS office expands horizons, connecting with like-minded peers and understanding real employer expectations firsthand.
- **Proactive Inquisitive Mindset:** Actively raising questions and engaging in debates during technical meetups unlocks knowledge hurdles that solo self-study cannot achieve.

### Applying to Internship Projects

- Apply enterprise AI architectural principles and AWS managed services to finalize our team project, **ImageGuard** (optimizing the image processing pipeline via S3 Events, AWS Lambda, and Amazon Rekognition).
- Actively prepare to participate in the upcoming **AWS Student Community Day Vietnam 2026**.
