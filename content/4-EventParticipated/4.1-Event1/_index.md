# Summary Report: “Buildrathon Kickoff: Code the Future with CMC Global”

### Event Information

- **Event Name:** Buildrathon Kickoff: Code the Future with CMC Global
- **Date & Time:** 09:00 – 12:00, Saturday, September 26, 2026
- **Location:** 26th Floor, Bitexco Financial Tower, 45 Ngo Duc Ke, Ben Nghe Ward, District 1, Ho Chi Minh City (AWS Vietnam Office)
- **Organizers:** First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ) Community in collaboration with CMC Global
- **Role:** Attendee

### Speakers & Guests

- **Mr. Huynh Hoang Long** – Event Host, Administrator of First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ) Community.
- **Cloud & AI Specialists from CMC Global** – Senior Solution Architects & Technical Leads.
- **Technical Mentors Team** – Supporting and guiding attendees during the Hands-on AI Agent Challenge.

### Event Objectives

- Explore the latest technological frontiers in AWS Cloud infrastructure, Generative AI, and modern Agentic AI architectures.
- Experience hands-on technical challenges by building a practical AI Agent prototype under direct expert mentorship.
- Gain career guidance for the Cloud & AI era through an insightful talk show with technology leaders from CMC Global.
- Foster high-quality networking with the First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ) community and find teammates for the Buildrathon.

### Key Highlights & Sessions

#### 1. Generative AI & AI Agents in Real-world Business Scenarios
- Comprehensive overview of modern AWS Cloud infrastructure powering next-generation Generative AI applications.
- The shift from conventional single-prompt LLMs to **Agentic AI** — autonomous agents capable of dynamic reasoning, multi-step planning, and tool/API execution.
- Real-world case studies shared by CMC Global on how leading enterprises deploy AI to automate operations and drive business efficiency.

#### 2. Talkshow: Accelerating Your Career in the Cloud & Generative AI Era
- **Self-positioning in the AI Age:** AI serves as an amplifier of capability (*AI Amplify*); however, strong core foundations in computer science and cloud architecture remain paramount.
- **Hiring Criteria from CMC Global:** What leading employers seek in fresh graduates and interns — proactive attitude, solid cloud/data foundation, adaptability, and hands-on exposure.
- **Career Roadmap:** How students and young professionals can build competitive advantages and navigate high-value tech opportunities.

#### 3. Hands-on Challenge: AI Agent Challenge
- Direct participation in designing and implementing an AI Agent prototype on AWS.
- Applied advanced Prompt Engineering alongside **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)** and **Knowledge Bases** to mitigate model hallucinations.
- Configured **Action Groups** to allow the AI Agent to seamlessly interact with real-world APIs and enterprise datasets.

#### 4. Ice-Breaking & Networking
- Engaging networking sessions connecting technology students with seasoned industry practitioners.
- Formed multidisciplinary project teams to prepare for the upcoming stages of the Buildrathon competition.

### Key Takeaways

- **Professional & Growth Mindset:** AI will not replace humans, but professionals who harness AI and understand cloud systems will outpace those who do not.
- **Broadening Value Perception:** Career growth encompasses not only financial remuneration but also network expansion, practical experience, and exposure to enterprise-scale systems.
- **Technical Architecture & Hands-on Capabilities:** Gained a solid understanding of the architectural components behind production-grade AI Agents on AWS: Foundation Models, Knowledge Bases, Function Calling, and Guardrails.
- **Structured Prompting & RAG:** Mastered structured prompting techniques and RAG integration patterns to ensure deterministic and accurate AI outputs.

### Applying to Work & Study

- **Adopting Agentic AI Thinking:** Break down complex software development requirements in internship projects into modular sub-tasks assigned to specialized AI agents.
- **Standardizing Prompts:** Structure prompts rigorously (Role, Instruction, Context, Constraints) to optimize token efficiency and response precision.
- **Reinforcing AWS Cloud Skills:** Continue executing hands-on labs within the FCAJ roadmap to be fully prepared for upcoming Buildrathon milestones.

### Event Experience

Attending the “Buildrathon Kickoff: Code the Future with CMC Global” workshop on the 26th floor of Bitexco Financial Tower was an inspiring and enriching milestone during my internship:
- Learning from Distinguished Industry Speakers: Speakers from CMC Global and Mr. Huynh Hoang Long delivered candid, pragmatic insights into the modern technology landscape and what enterprises evaluate beyond academic transcripts.
- Direct Hands-on Exposure: Building an AI Agent live during the workshop bridged the gap between theoretical knowledge and practical deployment, with technical mentors offering real-time debugging support.
- Professional Venue & High-Impact Networking: The modern venue on the 26th floor of Bitexco provided an inspiring learning atmosphere with panoramic city views, connecting me with ambitious peers across the FCAJ community.
