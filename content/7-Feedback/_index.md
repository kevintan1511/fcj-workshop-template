# Sharing and Feedback

Throughout my 3-month internship at **Amazon Web Services Vietnam Company Limited** within the **First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ)** program from **14/09/2026** to **14/12/2026**, I experienced an immensely valuable and inspiring learning journey. Below are my candid reflections, evaluations, and suggestions from the perspective of an Artificial Intelligence student at FPT University.

---

### Overall Program Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**
* The AWS Vietnam office on the 26th Floor of Bitexco Financial Tower offers a modern, highly professional, and inspiring workspace with panoramic city views.
* Providing real AWS accounts alongside dedicated hands-on credits completely bridges the gap between academic theory and enterprise cloud implementations.

**2. Support from Mentors and Program Organizers**
* The mentors (Mr. Lu Hoan Thien, Mr. Huynh Hoang Long) are dedicated, enthusiastic, and possess deep technical expertise.
* What I appreciated most is their inquiry-based mentoring philosophy: rather than handing out quick solutions, mentors pose guiding questions that motivate students to consult official architecture documentation (AWS Architecture Center), inspect error codes, and examine CloudWatch logs to master Root Cause Analysis.

**3. Relevance to Academic Major**
* As an Artificial Intelligence (AI) major, my prior exposure was largely focused on model training on local setups or Google Colab environments with static datasets.
* Participating in FCAJ made me realize that in enterprise environments, AI models deliver real value only when integrated into a robust cloud backbone: automated ingestion via S3 Event Notifications, serverless processing with AWS Lambda, secure distributed storage in DynamoDB, and granular access control via IAM and Presigned URLs.

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**
* I learned to think from the mindset of a Cloud Solutions Architect: aligning architectures with the 6 pillars of the AWS Well-Architected Framework, forecasting operating expenses using the AWS Pricing Calculator, automating cost safeguards with AWS Budgets, and drafting professional bilingual documentation.
* The capstone workshop project (**ImageGuard**) was the most impactful hands-on experience, allowing me to take an architecture from whiteboard diagrams through IAM policy refinement, debugging, and end-to-end verification.

**5. Company Culture and Team Spirit**
* A culture grounded in openness, mutual respect, and encouraging student proactiveness.
* Strong collaboration within **AITeamFPT**: when our initial proposal encountered service quota limitations, the team collectively evaluated alternatives and pivoted smoothly to Amazon Rekognition.
* Community tech events (Buildrathon Kickoff, AWS Cloud & AI Day Watch Party, Cloud Mastery 1) expanded my professional network and allowed me to exchange insights with seasoned engineers across Saigon.

**6. Internship Policies and Benefits**
* Interns enjoy access to AWS office facilities, 12 months of AWS Skill Builder Premium, hands-on cloud credits, and complimentary invitations to premium industry events.

---

### Additional Reflections

* **Most Satisfying Experience:**
  Watching our team's **ImageGuard** solution function seamlessly end-to-end from the web frontend: users upload images, the pipeline instantly moderates inappropriate content, indexes metadata, and dispatches real-time alerts via Amazon SNS without maintaining any physical servers.

* **Suggestions for Future Cohorts:**
  * *Earlier AI Lab Integration:* The initial weeks focus extensively on foundational networking (VPC) and compute (EC2). Introducing small labs on invoking managed AI APIs via Lambda around weeks 3–4 would help AI students maintain immediate relevance and enthusiasm for their major.
  * *Service Quota Awareness:* New accounts often have zero quota for certain advanced generative AI services (such as Bedrock) that require approval lead times. Providing early notifications or predefined fallback architectures will ensure smoother project execution.
  * *Regular Technical Office Hours:* Hosting weekly 45–60 minute virtual office hours on Discord or Google Meet would help interns quickly unblock common roadblocks like IAM permission errors or CORS issues.

* **Would I Recommend the Program to Friends?**
  * **Absolutely (100%):** I have been actively recommending FCAJ to junior students at FPT University. It is an exceptional program that not only equips students with cloud and AI capabilities but also instills international-standard professional work ethics.

---

### Suggestions & Future Outlook

* **Future Goals:** Immediately following the internship, I plan to leverage the provided Skill Builder Premium subscription to prepare for and achieve the **AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner** and **AWS Certified AI Practitioner** certifications to solidify my career trajectory.
* **Acknowledgments:** I express my sincere and deepest gratitude to the First Cloud AI Journey organizers, mentors at AWS Vietnam, and professors at FPT University for creating this transformative opportunity and supporting me throughout these 3 months.
