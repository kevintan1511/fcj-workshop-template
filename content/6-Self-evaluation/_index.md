# Self-Assessment

During my internship at **Amazon Web Services Vietnam Company Limited** (under the First Cloud AI Journey program) from **14/09/2026** to **14/12/2026**, I had a valuable opportunity to apply my academic foundation in Artificial Intelligence from FPT University into enterprise-grade cloud computing environments on AWS.

As a core member responsible for AI integration and technical workflows within **AITeamFPT**, I collaborated with my teammates to build and deploy the **ImageGuard** workshop – an automated image moderation and labeling system powered by Amazon Rekognition on a serverless architecture. This hands-on journey enabled me to gain exposure to practical concepts such as designing event-driven pipelines, enforcing least-privilege IAM policies, implementing image processing Lambda functions, conducting system testing, and authoring bilingual technical documentation.

Beyond technical competencies, I consistently maintained a serious work ethic, complied with office regulations, actively participated in AWS technology meetups at Bitexco, and kept an honest, comprehensive worklog reflecting real progress.

### Areas for Improvement

* **Cloud Infrastructure Depth:** Continue dedicating time to study advanced AWS networking and security services, as my prior background primarily focused on local AI model development.
* **Technical Presentation Skills:** Further practice articulating complex architectural flows and answering deep technical queries concisely, clearly, and confidently in front of larger groups.
* **Early Cost-Optimization Mindset:** Proactively plan cost estimation models and data lifecycle policies (S3 Lifecycle) during the initial design phase rather than addressing them toward the end.
* **Proactive Mentor Consultations:** When facing complex blockers (such as AI service quota constraints or advanced IAM permission boundaries), summarize findings and seek mentor guidance earlier to avoid spending excessive time debugging alone.
